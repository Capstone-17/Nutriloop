Serum Creatinine eGFR Blood Urea Potassium (K+) Sodium (Na+)
Essential for KDIGO CKD staging. Evaluates glomerular filtration rate, potassium arrhythmia safety floors, and fluid retention limits.
🩸 Glycemic & Metabolic Control
Diabetic Profile (HbA1c + Fasting Sugar)
HbA1c (%) Fasting Glucose (FBG) Postprandial (PPBS)
HbA1c represents your average blood glucose over the last 90 days (<5.7% Normal, 5.7–6.4% Prediabetes, ≥6.5% Diabetes). Guides carbohydrate and craving limits.

Systolic / Diastolic BP Height & Weight (BMI) Cholesterol
Establishes DASH sodium thresholds (Stage 1/2 Hypertension) and basal caloric budgeting derived from your Height & Weight measurements.
